# Security policy

## Supported versions

Only `main` is supported. Fixes land on `main` and go out in the next release.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Report it privately through GitHub: open the repository's
**Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include:

- what is affected (endpoint, configuration, version or commit);
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- the impact you expect;
- any fix or workaround you know of.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge a report within 5 working days.
- We keep you updated while we investigate and fix it.
- We agree a disclosure date with you once a fix is ready, and credit you if you want.

See [operations](docs/operations.md#security) for what EventStore does and does not protect.
